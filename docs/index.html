About Me

Thank you for visiting my website!

I earned my M.S. in Statistics from the University of New Mexico and my B.Sc. double major in Mathematics and Statistics from the University of Ghana. My academic journey reflects a commitment to applying statistical thinking and rigorous methodology to generate meaningful insights and support evidence-based decision-making.

Outside of my research, I engage in professional service through ASA and ENAR initiatives, support underrepresented high school and college students, enjoy connecting with nature, cooking authentic Ghanaian cuisine, participating in football, and pursuing hands-on craftsmanship, including making leather sandals and building wooden furniture.
